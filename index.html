COMMISSION TERMS

Kind, clear ground rules

These starter terms should be customized before accepting paid work.

Before work starts

A request is reviewed first. The artist confirms availability, scope, price, and timeline before a commission is accepted.

Payment

Use only the artist’s verified payment method after approval. Never email card numbers, account passwords, or payment screenshots with private information.

Changes

Reasonable changes at the agreed sketch stage are welcome. Major changes after approval may require an updated quote.

Timing & delivery

Timelines are estimates and depend on complexity and queue position. Finished work is delivered digitally in the agreed format.

Rights & credit

Personal-use commissions may be posted with artist credit. Commercial use needs written permission and a separate quote.

Portfolio & refunds

Clarify whether finished work may appear in the portfolio and add a specific refund policy before opening commissions.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How long does a commission take?

Timing depends on the piece and current queue. You will receive an estimated timeline when your request is approved—please customize this with your normal turnaround range.

When do I pay?

Only after the artist confirms the price and accepts your request. The artist will provide a verified payment method; this website does not collect card details.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes—reasonable edits during the agreed sketch stage are included. Bigger direction changes may affect the price or timeline.

What files will I receive?

Usually a high-resolution PNG or JPG. Ask before ordering if you need a specific format or size.

Can I use my commission commercially?

Commercial use is not automatically included. Let the artist know how you plan to use the artwork so they can provide the right quote and permission.
